Description

Patients with stage IV melanoma treated with anti-PD-1 alone (pembrolizumab or nivolumab) or combined with ipilimumab, at Melanoma Institute Australia and other centres: 75 patients with treatment, best response, tumour neoantigen counts and overall survival.

A small immunotherapy cohort that links tumour features to survival, useful for teaching survival analysis in oncology.

Known issues

Read these before analysis.

  1. Demonstration entry. Taken from the BioBench R package to show how HDR presents a dataset. It has not gone through HDR review and will be removed before launch.
  2. response is measured after treatment starts. Using it to predict survival introduces immortal time bias.
  3. Small sample (75 patients, 30 deaths).
  4. Neoantigen counts are missing for one patient.
