Description

Clinical records of 1,205 pregnant women seen at Kurigram General Hospital, a district hospital in rural northern Bangladesh: age, blood pressure, blood sugar, body temperature, body mass index, heart rate, medical history and a pregnancy risk level (low or high).

A recent, openly licensed set of routine antenatal measurements from a rural district hospital, built for the prediction of high-risk pregnancy.

Origin

Produced by Daffodil International University and Kurigram General Hospital

Published in Mojumdar MU et al. (2025). Maternal health risk factors dataset: clinical parameters and insights from rural Bangladesh. Data in Brief 59: 111363

Tables used Not stated

How obtained Taken from the BioBench R package (dataset highriskpreg), which took them from the biostatlab package, which took them from the Mendeley Data file. Column names were turned into snake_case and blank risk levels coded as missing.

Known issues

Read these before analysis.

  1. Demonstration entry. Taken from the BioBench R package to show how HDR presents a dataset. It has not gone through HDR review and will be removed before launch.
  2. Not the UCI Maternal Health Risk dataset. That older set (1,013 records, 7 columns) also comes from rural Bangladesh but is a different collection.
  3. Risk level is not defined. The authors say only that it was set from the clinical parameters; 18 records have no risk level.
  4. Some implausible values: one age of 325 years and one BMI of 0. 18 records duplicate another record apart from study_id.
  5. Collection dates are vague. The data article gives only 29 August 2023.
