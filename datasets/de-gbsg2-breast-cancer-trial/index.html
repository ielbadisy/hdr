Description

Patient records from the GBSG-2 randomised 2x2 trial of hormonal treatment and chemotherapy duration in node-positive breast cancer in Germany: tumour and patient characteristics with recurrence-free survival time.

A classic trial dataset for prognostic modelling in breast cancer.

Origin

Produced by German Breast Cancer Study Group

Published in Schumacher M et al. (1994). Randomized 2x2 trial evaluating hormonal treatment and the duration of chemotherapy in node-positive breast cancer patients. J Clin Oncol 12: 2086-2093

Tables used Not stated

How obtained Taken from the BioBench R package (dataset gbsg2), which took them from TH.data::GBSG2. Complete cases only (686 of 720 patients).

Published on HDR HDR-DE-0001, version 1.0.0

Origin address: https://doi.org/10.1200/JCO.1994.12.10.2086 (accessed 2026-11-02). Reuse terms: Distributed openly for research in the TH.data R package (GPL-3).

Known issues

Read these before analysis.

  1. Demonstration entry. Taken from the BioBench R package to show how HDR presents a dataset. It has not gone through HDR review and will be removed before launch.
  2. Complete cases only. 34 patients with missing values were dropped by the source.
