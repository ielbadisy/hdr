Description

Patients admitted with acute stroke to Bispebjerg Hospital, Copenhagen, in the Copenhagen Stroke Study: 518 patients with cardiovascular risk factors, stroke subtype, Scandinavian Stroke Scale score at admission and long-term survival.

A subset of a community-based stroke cohort, used in the pec package to show prediction error curves for Cox models and random survival forests.

Known issues

Read these before analysis.

  1. Demonstration entry. Taken from the BioBench R package to show how HDR presents a dataset. It has not gone through HDR review and will be removed before launch.
  2. A subset of the study. The full Copenhagen Stroke Study enrolled 1,197 patients; how these 518 were selected is not documented.
  3. Cholesterol units are not documented. Values suggest mmol/L.
