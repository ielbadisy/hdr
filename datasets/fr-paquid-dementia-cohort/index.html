Description

A sample of 2,561 people aged 65 and over from PAQUID, a prospective cohort on functional and cerebral ageing started in 1988 in Gironde and Dordogne, south-western France: two cognitive test scores at baseline and time to dementia or to death without dementia over up to 12 years.

A small extract of a landmark French ageing cohort, made public to show how baseline cognitive scores predict dementia when death competes with it.

Variables

paquid.csv

One row per participant

Variable Type Description
time number Years from baseline to dementia, death or censoring
status integer Event (0 censored, 1 dementia onset, 2 death without dementia)
DSST integer Digit Symbol Substitution Test score at baseline (attention and psychomotor speed)
MMSE integer Mini Mental State Examination score at baseline (0 to 30)

Known issues

Read these before analysis.

  1. Demonstration entry. Taken from the BioBench R package to show how HDR presents a dataset. It has not gone through HDR review and will be removed before launch.
  2. Only four columns. The sample has no age, sex or other covariates.
  3. Follow-up is censored at 12 years.
  4. Calendar dates are approximate. Coverage end is inferred from the 1988 start and 12 years of follow-up.
