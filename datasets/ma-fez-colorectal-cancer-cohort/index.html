Description

Retrospective cohort of 346 patients with colorectal cancer diagnosed and followed at Hassan II University Hospital, Fez, Morocco, between 2009 and 2015: sociodemographic, tumour, molecular and treatment characteristics, delay to start of treatment, and overall survival over 36 months from diagnosis.

One of the few openly available hospital cohorts of colorectal cancer in North Africa. It is suited to survival modelling at 36 months and to the study of delay to start of treatment.

Known issues

Read these before analysis.

  1. Demonstration entry. Taken from the BioBench R package to show how HDR presents a dataset. It has not gone through HDR review and will be removed before launch.
  2. Patient count differs in the article. The abstract reports 343 patients; the results section and this file have 346.
  3. Follow-up is capped at 36 months from diagnosis.
  4. Some values are missing: Delay (10), MSI.MSS.status (16), Braf.mutated and Ras.mutated (12 each), Histological.type (1).
  5. Braf.mutated has no positive case.
