Description

Clinical records and follow-up of 299 patients with left ventricular systolic dysfunction admitted to the Faisalabad Institute of Cardiology and the Allied Hospital, Faisalabad, Pakistan, in 2015, with time to death or censoring.

A small, complete cohort widely used to teach survival analysis and to compare prediction models.

Origin

Produced by Government College University and Faisalabad Institute of Cardiology

Published in Ahmad T et al. (2017). Survival analysis of heart failure patients: a case study. PLOS ONE 12(7): e0181001

Known issues

Read these before analysis.

  1. Demonstration entry. Taken from the BioBench R package to show how HDR presents a dataset. It has not gone through HDR review and will be removed before launch.
  2. Follow-up is short (4 to 285 days).
