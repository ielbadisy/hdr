Deposit a dataset

HDR is open to researchers everywhere. Deposit is free. You keep the credit; the community gets data it can trace, use and cite.

Before you start

You need three things:

  1. Your tables as CSV files, UTF-8, one table per file, with a header row.
  2. The origin of the data: who produced it, where it was published, and an address where it can be checked. No origin, no deposit.
  3. A metadata file, dataset.yml, describing the dataset, its origin and every column. The builder below writes it for you. You can also start from the blank template or look at a complete example.

Read the rules once: they are short, and they are the same for everyone.

How it works

Review takes up to 15 working days.

Metadata builder

Everything stays in your browser: nothing is sent until you choose to send it. Select your CSV files and the builder lists their columns for you to describe. Fields marked • are required.

Send your deposit


Send dataset.yml and your CSV files, zipped, to contact address to be added, with the subject “HDR deposit: your title”. We confirm receipt within two working days.


A web upload with automatic checks will replace e-mail once the first deposits from outside the team have gone through.






What happens next


    

  • The automatic checks run first. If something is missing, you get the list at once.
    • 

  • A curator checks the origin and three values chosen at random.
    • 

  • You see the dataset page and the PDF note before the DOI is minted, and you approve them.
    • 

  • After publication, you can deposit a new version at any time. Earlier versions and their DOIs remain.
