HDR · Health Data for Research

Open data for better science

A curated repository of health research data. Every dataset is traced to its source, reviewed by a curator, documented in a data note and assigned a DOI, so that analyses can be reproduced and data cited like publications.

Three files, one DOI, a known origin

Every dataset on HDR ships the same way, whoever deposits it.

CSV

The tables

One UTF-8 CSV per table, in long or wide format, ready for R, Python or Stata. Checksums published.

XLSX

The workbook

All tables in one Excel file, with an About sheet (origin, licence, citation) and a Variables sheet.

PDF

The dataset note

A few pages to read before the analysis: description, origin, every variable, known issues, checks against the origin, how to cite.
